How news affects trades

High-impact releases (interest-rate decisions, the US jobs report, inflation, GDP) can move a pair sharply within minutes and widen spreads.

Prices often whip both ways right after a release, so stops can be hit at worse prices than planned.

Safer habits: avoid opening new trades about 30 minutes before and after a high-impact event for your pair’s currencies, compare the actual result with the forecast once it’s out, and cut your position size or stay out if you’re unsure. Check each event’s time against your own time zone.
